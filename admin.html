Leave cover

For planned leave. The covering CRE sees and can update all of the absent CRE's orders for these dates – no requests needed. Cover ends automatically after the last day.

Reassign orders

Permanent. Use this when a CRE leaves, or to move a single order for good. Moving everything moves only open orders – delivered and returned orders stay with whoever handled them.
